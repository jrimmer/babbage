# 0079 Calculation forms the release notes lists that the engine lacks

Status: open
Milestone: v1
Area: forms

From the audit of the release notes (claims; the engine's answers were probed): `5 root of 2`; `2 exponent 4`, `4 raised to 2`; `min 5, 3, 7`, `max 50, 20, 10`, `gcd 10, 20, 5`, `max (100 * 2) 50 100`, `total of 3, 4, 5 7 and 9`, `min of 3 and 4` and `5 feet and 3 inches in cm` (`and` is read as a bitwise AND and refuses); `is 59 prime`; `1 in 20 as %`; `100 is what multiple of 50` and `multiple on` / `off` (the engine drops the words and prints 5,000); `half of half of 30% of 90`; `round 21 up to nearest 5`, `round down 20 to nearest 8`; `500 to 3000` as a change; `10:15 to decimal` (the engine prints 36,900); `1 liter in imperial pints` (the engine drops `imperial`); `1 1/2 pounds`; `200 oz in pounds & ounces`; `$3mn` (dropped); `USD1K`; `（2+3）×4` (full-width brackets); `100²`; `cos(60º)`; `5 + 5 # comment`; `hourly interest on $1k for 3 years at 4%`; `investment required for $10k/month at 5%`; `daily payment on $1M over 30 years at 6.5%`; `ppi of 15" screen at 1024x768`; `3 mph to minutes`; `8g/2 lb as grams/lb`; `3 hours / 4 days to hours / day`; `1 L / 1 m^2`. Done in this pass: the cotangent and cosecant functions and their variants, `multiple` as a word for `x`, `halfway between`. Several are quiet wrong answers and should be fixed before the rest.

Built since this was filed (pinned in the tests, values checked with mpmath and Python): `N root of X` (`5 root of 2` is 1.148698355), `is N prime` (up to 10^12, an error beyond), `A is what multiple of / on / off B` (`2x`, `0.5x`), `10:15 to decimal` (10.25; a time of day as a number is hours), `$3mn` for millions after a currency, and `1 liter in imperial pints` through new imperial gallon, quart, pint and fluid ounce units (`imperial pint`, `imp pt`, `uk pint`; three-word names such as `imperial fluid ounces` now lex). Still open from the list: `and` as a joiner (`min of 3 and 4`, `5 feet and 3 inches`), list functions without brackets, `1 in 20 as %`, `100²`, the full-width brackets, `USD1K`, `1 1/2 pounds`, `200 oz in pounds & ounces`, the finance phrases, `ppi of a screen`, `3 mph to minutes`.

Built since (the expected behaviour, A76 and A77): `min of 3 and 4` and `max of`, and `and` between the parts of one quantity (`5 feet and 3 inches`, `3 hours and 20 min`, `2 lb and 4 oz`). `100²` (A92) too.

Built in the audit-closing pass (each pinned in the tests, with the release notes's stated answer or a Python check): `2 exponent 4`, `4 raised to 2`, lists without `of` and separated by spaces (`min 5, 3, 7`, `gcd 10, 20, 5`, `max (100 * 2) 50 100`, `total of 3, 4, 5 7 and 9`), nested phrases (`half of half of 30% of 90`), `round 21 up to nearest 5`, `round down 20 to nearest 8`, `1 1/2 pounds`, `USD1K` and `EUR 50` (a currency code before an amount; `USD5` used to be `1 $⁵`), full-width brackets, `10500 in sci`, `1 in 20 as %`, `ppi of 15" screen at 1024x768`, `investment required for $10k/month at 5%`, `deposit needed for $42k/year at 7.5%`, `daily payment` and `interest paid` (a day is 365.2425 days there, as `$207.67` and `$116.40` need), `3 mph to minutes`, `10 cubic centimeters to meters`, `8g/2 lb as grams/lb`, `200 oz in pounds & ounces`. Still open: `hourly interest on $1k for 3 years at 4% compounding quarterly` (no stated answer, the meaning of an hourly interest figure is unclear), `5 squared`, `$500/month / 30 days`, the default pounds-and-ounces display of `13.5 lb`, `5 + 5 # comment` (the comment page lists no `#` style).
