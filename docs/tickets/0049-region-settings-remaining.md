# 0049 Region settings still to build

Status: open
Milestone: v1 (row 22)
Area: settings

Built: number format (three regions), underscore separators, the user's time zone, the work-hours setting, the region dollar (USD, AUD, CAD, NZD, SGD, HKD; `$` shows for the region's own dollar and `US$` for US dollars elsewhere) and US customary or imperial gallons, quarts, pints and fluid ounces. Still open: a metric volume system (its pint, quart and gallon values are not in the material available), imperial tablespoons and teaspoons (the cup, tablespoon and teaspoon stay US customary), and the rest of the dollar family (MXN, ARS and others). Also not covered: a leading decimal comma (`,5`) in Western Europe, Indian lakh and crore grouping, and a settings screen in the app (today a settings file is read).
