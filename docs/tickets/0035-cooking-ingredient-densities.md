# 0035 Cooking: volume and mass of ingredients

Status: open
Milestone: post-v1 (needs a shipped dataset)
Area: units, data

`300g butter in cups`, `10 cups olive oil in grams`: the maintainer's notes ships about 200 ingredient densities. The values are not in the maintainer's notes and have no single authority, so a vetted, cited dataset has to be assembled first. The conversion itself is trivial once a density is known: a user-defined density would work today as a custom unit. Also: oven temperatures and recipe scaling, which the page does not specify.
