# 0016 px at a pixel density

Status: built
Milestone: v1
Area: units

`1 cm in px at 326 ppi` (128.35 px) and `ppi`/`dpi`. The registry treats `px` as 1/96 inch (CSS). The `at <n> ppi` form needs its own phrase and a density unit.

Built: `1 cm in px at 326 ppi` and `326 px in cm at 326 dpi`, either direction, any whole or decimal density above zero; `px` alone stays 1/96 inch. The result's places are ticket 0055.
