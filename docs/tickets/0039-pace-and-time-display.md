# 0039 Pace and time display

Status: closed (the reference app and the reference note)
Milestone: v1 (row 19)
Area: time

`5 km in 25 minutes` gives a pace of `05:00/km` in the reference. Needs the timespan and laptime display formats.

Built: `5 km in 25 min` is `05:00/km` and `10 mi in 55 min` is `05:30/mi` (minutes and seconds per distance unit; `HH:MM:SS` when an hour or more).
