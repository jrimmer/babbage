# 0062 `Yesterday at` and `Tomorrow at` in time answers

Status: open
Milestone: v1
Area: time

The reference notes show `9:45 am - 15 hours 10 minutes | Yesterday at 6:35 pm` and `time in Thailand | Tomorrow at 12:30 am`. When a time of day lands on the day before or after today the answer names that day. The engine prints a bare `6:35 pm` for the first and `June 14 at 6:35 pm` style dated times elsewhere.
